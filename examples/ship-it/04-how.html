How it works

One binary, no build step.

  • Slides are plain HTML, served as-is in a sandboxed iframe.
  • deck.json is optional: title, ratio, notes.
  • Natural order: 2-intro before 10-end.
  • One WebSocket per session, three roles: stage, remote, viewer.
  • Works offline on the LAN, or through the Hub for the internet.
  • Written in Go, ships as a single file.
