# Deckhand compared

Deckhand turns a folder of HTML files, one per slide, into a presentation:
a stage on the big screen, a remote on your phone, a live link the audience
opens on theirs. One binary, works offline on the LAN, no account. The
hosted Hub at deckhand.show is optional.

Other tools do some of this, and several of them do other things much
better. These pages say which is which, without a sales pitch.

* [Deckhand vs reveal.js](/vs/reveal-js): the HTML presentation framework.
  Themes, plugins, auto-animate and PDF export on one side; one binary, a
  phone remote and a sandboxed slide per file on the other.
* [Deckhand vs Slidev](/vs/slidev): Markdown slides for developers on Vue and
  Vite. Great authoring, exports and a presenter mode you can open from a
  phone; Deckhand skips the toolchain and presents HTML you already have.
* [Deckhand vs Google Slides](/vs/google-slides): the collaborative editor.
  Co-editing, templates and audience Q&A against files you own, an offline
  LAN and an audience that follows live without an account.

If none of the three is your tool, the short version is the same: Deckhand
does not help you write slides, it presents the ones you have. The format is
in the [README](https://github.com/stranix79/deckhand#readme) and in
[FORMAT.md](FORMAT.md); a prompt to have a language model write a whole deck
is in [LLM.md](LLM.md).
